# Dr. Vittu OBDII Pro – Privacy Policy

## Overview

Dr. Vittu OBDII Pro ("the app") is developed by Dilip Kumar TD ("the developer", "we"). The app connects to an ELM327 OBD-II adapter in your car and shows live data, trips, fuel and service logs, fault-code diagnosis and road assistance.

The short version: your data stays on your phone. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDKs. The developer does not receive, collect or sell your personal data.

Last updated: 26 November 2026.

## Information the app handles

• Vehicle data from your OBD adapter: speed, RPM, temperatures, fuel level, fault codes, battery voltage and similar readings; vehicle name, VIN (read from the car or typed by you), registration number, insurance policy details, odometer and service details you enter.

• Vehicle documents you choose to add: photos or PDFs of papers such as the registration certificate, insurance policy, PUC certificate or licence, with their numbers and expiry dates. They are kept only in the app's private storage on your phone and included in backups you make.

• Precise location (GPS): used for trip routes, speed, the road assistant (speed limit, direction, hazards), weather, parking position and the petrol-station location of a fill-up.

• Fuel and receipt data you enter: litres, price, station name, notes and an optional receipt photo.

• Vehicle identity and documents you add: VIN (read from the car or typed), registration number, insurance details and photos/PDFs of papers such as the RC, insurance policy and PUC. They are kept only in the app's private storage and are included in backups you create.

• Bluetooth / Wi-Fi adapter details: the name and address of the OBD adapter you choose, so the app can reconnect.

• App settings, trip history, alerts and diagnosis results created by the app.

All of this is stored only on your device (in the app's private storage).

## How the information is used

• To show live gauges, record trips and routes, calculate economy and driving scores.

• To announce speed limits, speed breakers, cameras and school/hospital zones while you drive.

• To suggest possible causes of fault codes and maintenance reminders using on-device rules. No data is sent to an AI service.

• To create backups that you control.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, credit decisions or any sale to third parties.

## Location and background use

If "Road assistant" or background watch is on, the app keeps running with a visible notification and uses your location while the engine/adapter is active, even when the screen is off or the app is not on screen. This is needed for speed-limit announcements and trip recording. You can turn it off any time in Settings / Road assistant, or by denying the location permission.

## Services the app contacts

Some features need the internet. Only the minimum needed is sent, and none of it is sent to the developer:

• OpenStreetMap tile servers – map tiles for the coordinates you view (your IP address and the tile position are visible to them).

• Open-Meteo – approximate latitude/longitude to get current weather.

• Overpass API (OpenStreetMap) – coordinates near you to find the road speed limit, hazards and the nearest petrol station name.

• Your own MQTT broker / Home Assistant – only if you turn on Home Assistant in Settings. The app then sends live car readings (speed, RPM, temperatures, fuel, battery, odometer, fault-code count) and the car's GPS location to the broker address you enter. The developer never receives it; the broker's owner (usually you) does. The password is kept only on the phone and is left out of backups.

• Google Maps or another maps app – only when you tap Navigate; the destination is passed to that app, which has its own privacy policy.

These services have their own privacy policies. Their data is open data and may be inaccurate.

## Backups and cloud

• Backup & cloud lets you save one backup file (trips, routes, fuel log, receipts, documents, service, alerts, settings) to a location you pick – on the phone or in your own Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or similar. The file goes directly to the provider you choose under your account; the developer never sees it. Anyone with access to that file or account can read it, so protect it.

• Android automatic backup may copy app data to your Google account if you have Google backup turned on in Android settings. That is controlled by Google and your phone settings.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. Data leaves the app only when you choose to: sharing a report or trip, saving a backup, opening navigation, or when the services listed above are contacted. Information may be disclosed if required by law.

## Retention and deletion

Data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual drives, fill-ups, service records, documents and alerts by swiping them left, or delete everything with More → Privacy & legal → Delete all my data. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Backup files you saved elsewhere must be deleted by you in that location.

## Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage, protected by Android. Network requests use HTTPS. No method of storage is perfectly secure, and backups you place in cloud storage are protected only by that provider's security.

## Your choices and rights

You can use the app without granting optional permissions (the related features will not work), export your data using Backup, delete it as described above, and withdraw consent by turning features off or uninstalling. Depending on where you live (for example India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the GDPR) you may have rights to access, correct or erase your data. Because the developer holds no copy of your data, you can exercise these directly in the app.

## Children

The app is meant for drivers and vehicle owners and is not directed at children under 13 (or the age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect children's data.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app (More → Privacy & legal) and you may be asked to accept it again. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
